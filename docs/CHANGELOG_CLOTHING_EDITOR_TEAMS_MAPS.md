# Changelog: Clothing & Character Editor / Teams / Maps

Source: user-supplied changelog text (2026-11-07). No URLs present in the source.

## 👗 Clothing and character editor improvements
Clothing items now work better together, and many combinations look much better!
The character editor now shows which items are compatible with each other and which are not. You can still choose any combination you like.
Settings for color, base texture, smoothness, metallic, and custom properties have been moved to a separate window.
Your existing custom teams should be preserved, although the colors of some clothing items may change slightly after the update.

## 👚 New outfits
Around 150 new textures have been added for existing clothing. These are not new clothing models, but some textures dramatically change the original outfits and let you create entirely new looks!
The new looks include superheroines, girl scouts, futuristic suits, wasteland raiders, World War II nurses and pilots, princesses, gothic styles, military uniforms, a riding outfit, a sci-fi hacker look, a jungle outfit, several Japanese and fantasy outfits, Viking clothing including scale armor, and vintage-style lingerie, gloves, and boots.

## 👥 New teams
15 teams have been added with the new outfits. Some feature unique characters with their own backstories, so be sure to check out the team descriptions!
The Damsels in Distress team has also been updated. It is now a themed team with 6 main characters, each with a short backstory.

## 🌐 Team sharing and custom emblems
Introducing Team Hub, a full team-sharing system that lets you share teams without manually transferring files. You can publish any of your custom teams for other players to download.
You can now add your own descriptions and upload emblems for custom teams. Click Upload in the emblem selection window for instructions.
Emblems are shared along with teams through Team Hub and are also supported when importing and exporting team files. Teams can be imported regardless of the game version, although major updates may change their appearance or other settings.

## 🗺️ New maps
Three new maps have been added: Underground Caves, Prison Maze, and Pirate Outpost.
These beautiful, detailed maps support most game modes. Pirate Outpost is also the setting for two raids in Marooned.
There are also two new punishment maps: Forgotten Shore and Bandit Hideout.

## 🖥️ DLSS 5
If you are unfamiliar with DLSS, it is a feature for NVIDIA graphics cards that uses AI to improve in-game visuals and make them look more realistic.
I cannot add a simple in-game DLSS 5 on/off toggle yet, but there is an alternative: an external utility that is fairly straightforward to use. I tested it, and it worked.
I have posted the installation and setup guide on Discord: other-announcements
Once it is installed, you can turn the feature on or off through the utility's menu.

## ⚡ Clothing loading optimization
Clothing textures now load only when needed. This reduces RAM usage, but clothing may briefly appear pink when it first becomes visible.
You can disable this option in the graphics settings, but I do not recommend doing so if you have less than 25 GB of RAM.

## 🔌 Sex toy support
A Buttplug.io interface has been added for connecting sex toys. You can find it under Settings → Other, along with setup instructions.
You can customize how long connected devices run and how intensely they respond to different in-game situations.

## 🪑 Furniture settings
A separate furniture settings window is now available under Settings → Game → AI Behavior.
You can set the AI's priorities for individual pieces of furniture and adjust how often the new punishment animations are used.

## 🛠️ Other updates:
🔹 On Prison Complex, Bounty Hunt and Elimination now support battles with up to 6 teams instead of 4.
🔹 Repeatable island raids are now available after completing Marooned.
🔹 AI-controlled girls now use palm trees less often on the island in Marooned, as well as on other maps where there are too many of them.
🔹 AI-controlled girls no longer attack a bound player who is kneeling, whether or not the player can move. You can "pretend to be helpless" while trying to escape.
🔹 You can now voluntarily tie yourself to any piece of furniture for a set amount of time or indefinitely. This action is available in the F menu → Other Actions.
🔹 You can now open the full list of girls and their stats from the team information window.
