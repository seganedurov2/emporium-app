--- 🏫 NEW FACILITIES & SYSTEMS ---
* School:
    - New facility with 4 development levels.
    - Courses: Basic (Assimilation/Skills), Vocational (Specialized), 
      Predisposition, and Commercial.
    - "Trained at Emporium" status: Permanent bonuses to Exhaustion, 
      earnings, and market value.
    - Teacher management: Skill-based training, experience gain, and 
      exhaustion handling.
* Correction Center:
    - New facility for fear-based training.
    - Courses: Adaptation, Standard, Enhanced, and Intensive Correction.
    - Instructor system: Discipline/Charisma based effectiveness.
    - Integrated medical support for injuries sustained during correction.
* Hospital:
    - New facility with Medic and Surgeon positions.
    - Complete Wound System: 4 levels of severity (Scratch to Critical).
    - Surgical procedures: High-risk operations to treat severe wounds.
    - Recovery beds and emergency life-saving (First Aid) mechanics.
    - External patient revenue and plastic surgery services.
* Plastic Surgery:
    - 39 unique procedures (Facial, Body, Genital, Scar revision).
    - Multi-stage operations and recovery periods.
    - Impact on "Body Naturalness" and Attractiveness.

--- 🛠 CORE SYSTEMS ---
* Wounds & Scars:
    - Individual tracking of wound location and severity.
    - Incapacitation mechanics: characters cannot work at 25+ injury points.
    - Permanent scars affecting market value and health.
* Interface & Events:
    - New event "An Hour After Closing" and "Overstepping Authority".
    - Animated main menu logo and atmosphere effects.
    - Improved Assistant notifications with direct app linking.

--- ⚖️ BALANCE & FIXES ---

* Fixed text rendering for CJK languages on Linux.
* Fixed Attractiveness calculation to include Exhaustion and Wound penalties.

[VERSION 0.2.1 - PREVIOUS RELEASE]

--- 🛠 CORE ARCHITECTURE ---
* Implementation of a cross-platform web-app using React 19 and Vite.
* Integrated Capacitor for Android deployment (native android/ folder).
* State management powered by Zustand.
* UI styling implemented with Tailwind CSS v4.

--- 📈 ECONOMY & GAME ENGINE ---
* Global Market System:
    - Implementation of 21 distinct geographic regions.
    - Dynamic pricing based on regional demand (0.15 to 0.95 multipliers).
    - Tiered quality system: Low, Medium, and High/Elite.
    - Arbitrage mechanics: Route optimization from low-cost sources (e.g., Central Africa) to premium markets (e.g., North Africa).
* Character Generation Engine:
    - complex quality score formula: (Submission * 0.6 + Skill * 0.4).
    - Dynamic trait generation (Health, Exhaustion, Loyalty).
    - High-tier ("Elite") character specifications for luxury venues.

--- 🎨 ASSET PIPELINE ---
* Automated Portrait System:
    - Developed `tools/generate-portraits.mjs` for AI-driven character visuals.
    - Integration with multiple providers: OpenAI (DALL-E 3), Pollinations (Flux/SDXL), Ollama, and SD WebUI.
    - Support for adult-themed aesthetics ("bdsm" style) and precise framing (Head, Chest, Three-quarter, Full-body).
    - Automatic manifest synchronization via `src/portraitAssets.ts`.
    - Image processing pipeline using `sharp` for WebP optimization.

--- 📦 PROJECT STRUCTURE ---
* /src          - Core game logic, engine, and UI components.
* /public       - Static assets and regional portrait libraries.
* /tools        - Automation scripts for content generation.
* /android      - Native Android wrapper configuration.
* MARKET_DATA.md - Comprehensive export of economic balances and formulas.
